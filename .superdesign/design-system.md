# Rahidul Islam Portfolio Design System

## Direction
Professional minimal 2026 employer-facing portfolio for a midlevel Python/Django engineer in Bangladesh targeting Germany. Calm, credible, technical, direct.

## Tokens
- Canvas: #fafbfc white/off-white
- Ink: #172b3a navy
- Teal: #176b64 muted teal
- Rule: #dce3e8
- Muted text: #596773
- Type: system sans only, no serif display faces
- Spacing: generous 8px rhythm; wide margins and thin rules
- Shape: restrained cards, 6px radius, no decorative symbols or gradients

## Components
- Slim top navigation with monogram/name, section anchors, and compact availability note
- Hero with name, "Python & Django Developer", Bangladesh, Germany-targeting context, and concise CTA
- Text-based project cards: TalentBridge, Smart Document Vault, HotelMotel, Portfolio
- Experience timeline with years, company, role, and one-line scope
- Expertise matrix with Django/Python, APIs, data, delivery
- Footer CTA with email and LinkedIn/GitHub links

## Voice
Specific, direct, grounded. Never invent metrics, employers, visa status, languages, or client outcomes.
